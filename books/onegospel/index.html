    简介 Introduction

    新约之世，马太、马可、路加和约翰皆为拿撒勒人耶稣作传，自成体系，独具一格。虽递相参差，却各有千秋，可谓不谋而合，异曲同工。逐一品味，仔细查考，仍当奉为研经之圭臬。

    虽然，本书另辟蹊径，总摄耶稣事迹，囊括福音四卷，立足恢弘之视角，采用铺叙之手法，旨在复呈耶稣至暂之生平，再现基督极重之救恩，其生死苏升，其言行举止，其公义无瑕，其大爱悲悯，其神迹，其异能，至圣至荣，可畏可颂，犹如全景画幅，遍览无遗。是故，一书在手，可以整体阅之而不必留意诸色。诸色者，所以辨识字句溯洄源流也。合而参之，谨且慎之，不亦无失神启之精髓，而得福音之大观乎？此其一也。

    不惟如此，端赖诸色之标识，读者亦可置全局而顾一隅，聚焦色彩之经纬，沿袭篇章之脉络，徜徉其中，出入自在，或探字句之究竟，或察各卷之贡献，恰似领略山河之表里，又如欣赏锦绮之艺术。此其二也。

    仅举一例以作示范。有关我主升天之形状，见于马可福音十六章十九节，及路加福音廿四章五十至五十一节，其记曰：“主耶稣和他们说完了话，领他们到伯大尼的对面，就举手给他们祝福。后来正祝福的时候，他就离开他们，被接带到天上去了，坐在神的右边。”

    以上合记一气呵成，毫无违和之感。然，若以色彩区隔而分别读之，则易如反掌耳。试视暗黄及蓝绿（乃马可与路加共同之色）之字句，马可记曰：“主耶稣和他们说完了话，后来被接到天上，坐在神的右边。”

    再视红色及蓝绿之字句，路加则记曰：“耶稣领他们到伯大尼的对面，就举手给他们祝福。正祝福的时候，他就离开他们，被带到天上去了。”

    然而，本书十余万字，并非句句如此直白。四卷皆涉之篇章，单色有之，复色亦有之，单复缀连，彼此交错，繁简相异，难易有殊。甚且，事情之先后，语序之选择，句读之考量，重字之删缩，琐略种种，不一而足。因此之故，虽经笔者呕心沥血，斟酌再三，然以单色而论，终难复述各卷而字字保留，未免千虑一失之患。当然，笔者初衷，非举神启而欲篡之，实乃冀以新颖之法，发扬真理之光，而无损于正典之权威也。

    主后百六十年，叙利亚之塔蒂安（Tatian）撰《四福音合编》（Diatessaron），意将四卷福音合而为一。其成书也，未循任一原典之时序；若遇平行段落，则择其一而增以其余。如一古抄本跋曰：“赖神之助，福音圣书至此结束。此书也，由塔蒂安执笔，纂自四卷福音书，故名曰《四福音合编》。颂赞归神，因祂是主，唯其是配。荣耀归之，爰及世世。”以当时之技，塔氏竭其所能，成此合编，以飨叙利亚之弟兄，无意取四卷福音而代之，却以特有之方传达佳美之讯息，更策励同侪熟识耶稣并效仿之。

    本书得以杀青，内人Holly及李书杰、姚能亮二兄功不可没，我将铭于心而永不忘。然而，我所至为感激者，当属厚赐百物之神，天时地利人和，三者皆备，方成此廿一世纪之四福音合参。真理之道，唯独耶稣，诸君勉之，归于善乎！

    This work was written with the goal that you will be able to read the comprehensive account of Jesus of Nazareth, as described in the Gospels of Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John, in multiple ways. One way is to read it as a cohesive whole without necessarily noting the colors which identify what particular Gospel or combination of Gospels each word, sentence, paragraph, and section comes from. Although reading the Gospels individually remains the gold standard of Bible study, seeing all of the information from all four sources at once gives a fuller picture of miraculous acts, dialogue, and teaching. As Christians, we believe that God gave us the components. Did he not then give us the whole, as long as we are careful not to distort His words?

    Another way to use this work is to read the words while noting the colors. It will not take long until you appreciate how the four Gospels integrate with each other, like the threads of a beautiful tapestry. At the same time, you can look at a section and see all of the individual contributions of each Gospel. The color key will guide you in this.

    Here’s a relatively simple example, the description of the ascension of our Lord, in Mark 16:19 and Luke 24:50-51: Truly then, after the Lord had spoken to them, he led them out as far as Bethany, and he lifted up his hands and blessed them. It came to pass, while he blessed them, he was parted from them and taken up to the heaven, and sat by the right hand of God.

    The entire sentence can be read as one coherent whole. However, you could also just read the sections contributed by Mark fairly easily by reading the sections that are dark yellow (Mark) and teal (Mark and Luke). So, Mark’s contribution is: Truly then, after the Lord had spoken to them, he was taken up to the heaven, and sat by the right hand of God. Likewise, you could also just read Luke’s contribution by reading the sections that are red (Luke) and teal (Mark and Luke). Luke’s contribution is: He led them out as far as Bethany, and he lifted up his hands and blessed them. It came to pass, while he blessed them, he was parted from them and taken up to the heaven.

    Every sentence is not as straightforward as this. In sections where all four Gospels contribute to a narrative, it will require looking at several combinations of colors to read one Gospel’s contribution. Additionally, it is not possible to be able to exactly recreate each individual Gospel as it was given by reading every sentence like the above example sequentially. Innumerable choices had to be made, including choices of word order, verb tense, which words should be condensed, and what is the sequence of the events of Jesus’ life. I can assure you this was done with the heart of a believer that has no desire to alter God’s word. But I can also tell you that although God has given us the whole just as much as he has given us the components, I cannot claim that this presentation is inerrant. However, I can tell you that no single content word of any of the four Gospels has been left out of this Gospel Harmony deliberately, and to the extent that it faithfully conveys the four Gospels, it conveys the truth in a way that is unique.

    Around 160 AD, Tatian wrote the Diatessaron, which literally means “out of four.” It was a Gospel harmony that used nearly all of the four Gospels. He did not follow the chronology of any of the four Gospels, and for parallel passages, he would choose one of the Gospels as the source, and add selected verses from other accounts. An ancient manuscript of this work was given the following postscript by a scribe: “Here ends, by the help of God, the holy Gospel that Titianus compiled from the four Gospels, which is known as Diatessaron. And praise be to God, as he is entitled to it and Lord of it! And to him be the glory forever.” Tatian did the best he could with the technology he had available, and shared it with his Assyrian brothers, not to replace the true Gospels with his work, but to present the Good News in a unique way, and to inspire them to learn more about Jesus and equip them to follow Him.

    绪言 Preface

    神的儿子，耶稣基督福音的起头。

    路加写道，“提阿非罗大人哪，有好些人提笔作书，述说在我们中间所成就的事，是照传道的人从起初亲眼看见又传给我们的。这些事我既从起头都详细考察了，就定意要按着次序写给你，使你知道所学之道都是确实的。”

    The beginning of the gospel of Jesus the Messiah, the Son of God.

    Luke writes “seeing that many have taken in hand to set in order a narrative of those things which have been fulfilled among us, even as they, who from the beginning were eyewitnesses and servants of the word, delivered them to us, it also seemed good to me, having followed all things diligently from the very beginning, to write to you in order, most excellent Theophilus, so that you might know the certainty of those things, in which you have been instructed.”

    耶稣降生之前即已存在 Jesus’ Existence Before His Birth

    太初有道，道与神同在，道就是神。这道太初与神同在。万物是借着他造的；凡被造的，没有一样不是借着他造的。生命在他里头，这生命就是人的光。光照在黑暗里，黑暗却不接受光。

    他在世界，世界也是借着他造的，世界却不认识他。他到自己的地方来，自己的人倒不接待他。凡接待他的，就是信他名的人，他就赐他们权柄做神的儿女。这等人不是从血气生的，不是从情欲生的，也不是从人意生的，乃是从神生的。道成了肉身，住在我们中间，充充满满地有恩典有真理。我们也见过他的荣光，正是父独生子的荣光。

    In the beginning was the word, and the word was with God, and the word was God. The same was with God in the beginning. All things were made by him, and no created thing was made without him. In him was life, and the life was the light of men. The light shone in the darkness, and the darkness did not comprehend it.

    He was in the world, and the world was made through him, and the world did not know him. He came to his own, and his own did not receive him. But as many as did receive him, to those who believe in his name, to them he gave authority to become the sons of God. They were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God.

    The word became flesh and dwelt among us. We saw his glory, like the glory of the only begotten of the Father, full of grace and truth.

    约翰为耶稣作见证于伯大尼 John testifies about Jesus in Bethabara

    约翰所作的见证记在下面。犹太人从耶路撒冷差祭司和利未人到约翰那里，问他说：“你是谁？”

    他就明说，并不隐瞒，明说：“我不是基督。”

    他们又问他说：“这样，你是谁呢？是以利亚吗？”他说：“我不是。”“是那先知吗？”他回答说：“不是。”于是他们说：“你到底是谁？叫我们好回复差我们来的人。你自己说你是谁？”他说：“我就是那在旷野有人声喊着说：‘修直主的道路’，正如先知以赛亚所说的。”那些人是法利赛人差来的。他们就问他说：“你既不是基督，不是以利亚，也不是那先知，为什么施洗呢？”约翰回答说：“我是用水施洗，但有一位站在你们中间，是你们不认识的，就是那在我以后来的，我给他解鞋带也不配。”这是在约旦河外伯大尼，约翰施洗的地方作的见证。

    次日，约翰看见耶稣来到他那里，就说：“看哪，神的羔羊，除去世人罪孽的！这就是我曾说：‘有一位在我以后来、反成了在我以前的，因他本来在我以前。’我先前不认识他，如今我来用水施洗，为要叫他显明给以色列人。”约翰又作见证说：“我曾看见圣灵仿佛鸽子从天降下，住在他的身上。我先前不认识他，只是那差我来用水施洗的对我说：‘你看见圣灵降下来，住在谁的身上，谁就是用圣灵施洗的。’我看见了，就证明这是神的儿子。”

    This is the testimony of John, when the Jews sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem to ask him, "Who are you?"

    He confessed and did not deny, "I am not the Messiah."

    They asked him, "What then? Are you Elijah?"

    And he said, "I am not."

    "Are you the prophet?"

    He answered, "No."

    Then they said to him, "Who are you, that we may give an answer to those who sent us? What do you say concerning yourself?"

    He said. "I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness, 'Make straight the way of the Lord' as the prophet Isaiah said."

    Those who were sent were from the Pharisees. They asked him, "Why do you baptize then, if you are not the Messiah, nor Elijah, neither the prophet?"

    John answered them, "I baptize with water, but there stands among you, one whom you do not know. He who is coming after me is preferred before me, whose sandal strap I am not worthy to loosen.”

    These things came to pass in Bethabara, beyond Jordan, where John was baptizing.

    The next day John saw Jesus coming to him, and said, "Look! The lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world. This is he concerning whom I said, 'After me comes a man who is preferred before me, for he was before me.' And I did not know him, but only that he might be manifested to Israel. Because of this I came baptizing with water."

    And John bore witness, saying, "I saw the Spirit descending from heaven like a dove, and it remained on him. I did not know him, but he who sent me to baptize with water said to me, 'On whom you see the Spirit descending, and remaining on him, this is he who baptizes with the Holy Spirit.' And I have seen, and bore witness, that this is the Son of God."
